## 📑 Executive Summary & Business Intelligence Role

The **Power BI Executive BI Portal (`NYC_Carbon_Heist_Mitigation_PowerBI_Dashboard.pbix`)** serves as our enterprise-grade **Pillar 6 Visual Analytics Engine** alongside Tableau Desktop. Designed specifically for corporate boardroom presentations, municipal oversight audits, and C-Suite capital strategy meetings, it transforms raw building energy disclosures (`sample_nyc_energy.xlsx` / `sample_nyc_energy.csv`) across **11,638 audited NYC properties** into an immersive, interactive 3-page visual decision-support system.

By combining synchronized slicer panels (`Borough`, `Property Type`, `Decade Built`) with high-contrast Dark Royal Navy aesthetics (`#0F172A`), glowing Electric Cyan bars (`#38BDF8`), and financial waterfall accounting (`#22C55E` / `#38BDF8`), this dashboard enables stakeholders to instantly isolate high-emitting building clusters, evaluate Local Law 97 statutory penalty exposure (**$2.83 Billion baseline liability**), and trace exact self-funding decarbonization retrofits (**$640.9 Million in fine reduction**).

## 🏛️ 3-Page Executive Storytelling Architecture

The `.pbix` workbook is structured into a progressive 3-page analytical narrative designed to answer the three critical executive questions: *Where are our emissions concentrated? Which physical archetypes drive our liability? And how do we strategically deploy capital to mitigate penalties?*

### 📄 Page 1: Overview & Factors Affecting GHG Emissions
Focuses on macro-level portfolio distribution and primary emission drivers across all 11,638 properties (`10.56M tCO₂e` total emissions):
- **Executive KPI Command Bar:**
  - `10.56M tCO₂e` — Total Greenhouse Gas Emissions across all five boroughs.
  - `907 tCO₂e` — Average Annual Emissions per property.
  - `11.639K` (`11,638`) — Total Audited Building Records anchored across relational tables.
  - `62.1` — Portfolio Average ENERGY STAR Benchmarking Score.
- **Total Emissions by Property Type:** Horizontal ranked bar chart revealing **Multifamily Housing** (`~5.40M tCO₂e`) and **Commercial Office** (`~1.75M tCO₂e`) as the two overwhelming contributors (~68% of total portfolio emissions), followed by `Hospital (General Medical & Surgical)` (`~0.50M tCO₂e`), `K-12 Schools`, and `Hotels`.
- **Average Emissions Trend by Building Area:** Dynamic line plot demonstrating exponential emission escalation as building gross floor area (`GFA`) crosses the `10M sq. ft.` and `20M+ sq. ft.` thresholds.
- **Emissions Distribution by ENERGY STAR Rating:** Vertical distribution profiling that exposes a massive spike of high emissions (`~1.9K tCO₂e`) concentrated at unrated or poor `ENERGY STAR Score = 0` properties, emphasizing the immediate need for Level 2 energy efficiency audits.
- **Electricity Share vs. Gas Share by Borough:** 100% stacked bar comparison contrasting fossil-fuel heating dependence (`Gas Share` `#2563EB`) against grid electrification (`Electricity Share` `#93C5FD`) across `Manhattan`, `Outside NYC`, `Staten Island`, `Queens`, `Brooklyn`, and the `Bronx`.

### 📄 Page 2: Deep-Dive Asset Granularity & Construction Era
Provides granular drilling into specific high-liability buildings, construction age cohorts, and geographic distributions:
- **Executive KPI Command Bar:**
  - `10.56M tCO₂e` — Total Portfolio Greenhouse Gas Emissions.
  - `Manhattan` — Identified as the **Highest Emitting Borough** in NYC.
  - `11.639K` — Total Building Count.
  - `62.1` — Average ENERGY STAR Score.
- **GHG Emissions vs. Building Area by Construction Decade:** Dual-axis combination chart mapping total decade emissions (`1960s` peaking at `~1.6M tCO₂e` and `1920s` at `~1.4M tCO₂e`) against average building footprint, proving that post-war mid-century (`1950s–1970s`) and pre-depression (`1910s–1930s`) structures represent the primary engineering challenge.
- **GHG Emissions by Borough (Treemap):** Proportional spatial treemap illustrating exact regional contribution:
  - **Manhattan:** `5.40M tCO₂e` (`~51.1%` of citywide footprint — Bright Cyan `#0EA5E9`).
  - **Brooklyn:** `1.75M tCO₂e` (`~16.6%` — Deep Navy `#1E3A8A`).
  - **Bronx:** `1.54M tCO₂e` (`~14.6%` — Orange `#EA580C`).
  - **Queens:** `1.43M tCO₂e` (`~13.5%` — Maroon/Red `#BE123C`).
  - **Staten Island:** `0.71M tCO₂e` (`~6.7%` — Rose `#F43F5E`).
- **Total Emissions by Property Name:** Individual asset ranking isolating the top municipal liabilities, led by **`Co-Op City`** (`~150K tCO₂e/yr`), **`Pratt S.I. Campus - Total`** (`~135K tCO₂e/yr`), **`Spring Creek Towers`** (`~100K tCO₂e/yr`), and major medical centers (`NYP Columbia`, `NYP Cornell`).

### 📄 Page 3: Strategic Decarbonization Waterfall (`Carbon Cost & Savings Analysis`)
Synthesizes engineering interventions with financial penalty accounting under NYC Local Law 97 statutory thresholds:
- **Financial & Regulatory KPI Command Bar:**
  - `10.56M tCO₂e` — Baseline Portfolio Emissions.
  - **`$2.83 Billion` (`$2,830.3M`)** — **Estimated Carbon Penalty:** Total cumulative baseline statutory liability facing non-compliant properties across the 2024–2050 regulatory compliance horizons (`$268/MT` excess charge).
  - **`$641 Million` (`$640.9M`)** — **Total Fine Savings:** Cumulative financial savings unlocked across the 6 engineering retrofits.
  - **`$0.23 / sq. ft.`** — **Highest Penalty Property Intensity:** Maximum per-square-foot regulatory penalty across critical risk properties.
  - `62.1` — Average ENERGY STAR Score.
- **Estimated Carbon Cost Reduction Scenario (Waterfall Chart):** Sequential financial bridge tracking liability mitigation from baseline down to residual net exposure:
  1. **`Baseline Liability`:** **`$2,830.3M`** (`$2.83 Billion` starting exposure — Cyan bar).
  2. **`Surgical Strike` (Playbook 1):** **`-$20.55M`** savings (LED lighting retrofits and smart thermostat schedules — Emerald Green step).
  3. **`Smart Scale` (Playbook 2):** **`-$85.23M`** savings (Variable Frequency Drives (`VFDs`) and Building Management System (`BMS`) optimization).
  4. **`WET Recovery` (Playbook 3):** **`-$117.89M`** savings (Water/Energy/Thermal system recovery, steam trap overhauls, and pipe insulation).
  5. **`Electrification Push` (Playbook 4):** **`-$176.89M`** savings (Fossil-fuel boiler phase-out and commercial air-to-water heat pump installations).
  6. **`RCx Upgrades` (Playbook 5):** **`-$240.37M`** savings (Deep Retro-commissioning, window glazing replacement, and advanced envelope sealing).
  7. **`Total Residual Net Liability`:** **`$2,189.37M`** (`$2.19 Billion` remaining post-retrofit penalty exposure — Cyan anchor).

## 🎨 Design System & Visual Aesthetics

The Power BI dashboard enforces modern **C-Suite visual excellence and UI/UX best practices**:

- **Deep Royal Navy Theme (`#0F172A` / `#1E1B4B`):** Provides a sophisticated, low-glare dark background that eliminates visual fatigue during long boardroom reviews while making high-contrast data series pop.
- **Electric Cyan & Sapphire Highlighting (`#38BDF8` / `#0EA5E9`):** Used consistently for baseline metrics, property type bars, and residual totals to ensure instant visual scannability.
- **Financial Waterfall Coding (`#22C55E` vs. `#38BDF8`):** Strict semantic color-coding where positive liability reductions (`Savings`) render in vibrant **Emerald Green (`#22C55E`)**, while static baseline/total anchors remain **Electric Cyan (`#38BDF8`)**.
- **Synchronized Global Slicers Pane:** A persistent left-hand navigation card featuring rounded dropdown slicers for **`Borough`**, **`Property Type`**, and **`Decade Built`**. Filtering on any slicer instantly updates all charts and KPI cards across the current view.

## 🛠️ Data Model & DAX Architecture

- **Data Source Extract Binding:** The workbook imports data directly from `data/sample_nyc_energy.xlsx` (`sample_nyc_energy.csv`), containing `11,638` audited building records across `24+` cleaned engineering variables (`Site EUI`, `Source EUI`, `Total GHG Emissions`, `GFA`, `ENERGY STAR Score`).
- **DAX Measures & KPIs:** Embeds robust Data Analysis Expressions (`DAX`) for dynamic aggregation across multi-dimensional filtering contexts:
  - `Total GHG Emissions = SUM('sample_nyc_energy'[Total GHG Emissions])`
  - `Average ENERGY STAR = AVERAGE('sample_nyc_energy'[ENERGY STAR Score])`
  - `Estimated Carbon Penalty = SUM('sample_nyc_energy'[Base LL97 Penalty])`
  - `Total Fine Savings = SUM('sample_nyc_energy'[Penalty Savings Potential])`

## 🚀 Navigation & Local Execution Guide

To open and present the standalone visual BI dashboard locally during executive or engineering reviews:

1. **Prerequisites:** Ensure you have **Microsoft Power BI Desktop** installed (Windows 10/11 x64, version 2024+ recommended). Download free from [Microsoft Power BI Desktop Store](https://powerbi.microsoft.com/desktop/).
2. **Launch Workbook:** Navigate to the `carbon-heist-mitigation/PowerBI/` folder and double-click `NYC_Carbon_Heist_Mitigation_PowerBI_Dashboard.pbix`.
3. **Explore Pages:** Use the bottom page tabs in Power BI (`Factors Affecting Greenhouse Gas Emissions`, `Factors Affecting Greenhouse Gas Emissions` - Tab 2, and `Carbon Cost & Savings Analysis`) to navigate through the executive story.
4. **Interactive Filtering:** Click any dropdown in the left-hand **Slicers Pane** (`Borough`, `Property Type`, `Decade Built`) or click directly on any bar chart / treemap sector to cross-filter the entire dashboard dynamically.
